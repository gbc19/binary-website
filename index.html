About Binary

Connect with the voices behind your favorite characters

Binary brings fans and voice actors together. We’re building a place to discover the performers behind your favorite characters, request personalized audio or video messages, and share them with someone special.

Features

What you can look forward to

Discover performers

Find the voice actors behind your favorite animated characters.

Filter by interest

Browse by genre or see which characters are trending and getting the most requests.

Follow voice actors

Keep up with convention dates, appearances, and upcoming projects.

Personalized requests

Ask for an audio or video message in a tone or style of your choosing.

Live conversations

Request a one-on-one phone or video conversation with a voice actor.

Send a gift

Choose a message for a friend or family member and send it straight to them.

Benefits

Made for sharing

Make someone’s day

Send birthday wishes, words of encouragement, or a funny message. The recipient can enjoy it without downloading the app.

Share a favorite moment

Share completed requests and conversations directly to social media.

Keep in touch

Create a fan profile and leave a note on a performer’s page after an interaction.
